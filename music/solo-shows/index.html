Solo Shows

Vocals & Guitar

I'm a singer and songwriter who plays crowd-pleasing cover sets around southeastern, WI. From Blondie and Whitney Houston to Adele and Coldplay, I bring an engaging vocal performance that invites listeners to stay awhile and enjoy their favorite songs. Some of the past venues I've played include Summerfest, the Milwaukee County Zoo, Great Lakes Distillery, and The Landing Beer Garden at Hoyt Park.

For bookings email roloff.sheri@gmail.com.
