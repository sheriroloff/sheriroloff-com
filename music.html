Music Projects

I'm a singer/songwriter and multi-instrumentalist who loves to record and play live. Check out the video below to hear clips from my solo performances, and keep scrolling to learn more about my music projects.

Acoustic duo: Hey Tiger

Hey Tiger is an acoustic duo with my friend and talented artist Julia Keuler. We perform a fun blend of harmony-rich pop-rock covers. Take a listen and see where we're playing next at HeyTigerBand.com.

Bring Us the Stars

Bring Us the Stars was a long-distance recording project we often described as “haunted house rock.” We made two EPs in the wake of COVID. Take a listen at BringUsTheStars.com.

Mercury Crossing

Mercury Crossing is an alt-rock trio that played original music at a variety of venues from dive bars to multiple stages at Summerfest. We're no longer an active band, but it's how I met the drummer and love of my life Owen Strombeck. Take a listen to our final album on Bandcamp.
